# Bubble 3D visual test

Open `Assets/Scenes/BOKS_Bubble3D_Test.unity` directly and press Play.
The scene creates a preview camera and the existing BOKSBubble3D prototype at runtime.

- Click/tap the bubble or press **Scoppia**.
- Press **Ripristina** to repeat, including during a pop.
- Compare the new membrane tear with the original burst using the toggle. A click starts the tear at the point of contact; the Scoppia button starts it at the front centre.
- Compare light/dark backgrounds, mist duration, wobble, rim opacity, iridescence and highlights.
- Values changed during Play are temporary.

Use a landscape Game View for the most comfortable comparison. No menu or campaign progression is required. The scene is not added to player Build Settings.

The new mode opens an irregular hole in the rendered membrane in about 0.18 seconds and emits a few tiny droplets. The original burst remains available for comparison. The sandbox does not connect the effect to campaign gameplay.
